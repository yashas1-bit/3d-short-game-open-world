FIELD GUIDE

CONTROLS

WASD / arrows — Move
Mouse — Rotate camera (click game to lock pointer)
Left click / J — Chain sword combos
Q — Timed parry (brief window)
R — Shadow burst (costs stamina)
Space — Dodge roll
E — Collect loot / claim boss relics
Esc — Pause
Campaign — Clear the chapter patrol before the boss becomes vulnerable. Your progress saves locally. Secret cache crystals appear when you explore off the main path.
